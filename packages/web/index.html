Model & constraints

Reduced memory recomputes backward blocks and disables the sampling cache. Use it when memory is more important than repeated-generation speed. Greedy is not optimization

        

          If P(a)=0.60 and P(b)=0.40, greedy chooses a. But a splits equally
          into ax and ay, each with probability 0.30. If b always continues to
          x, bx is the most probable complete sequence at 0.40. Exact sampling
          explores all three with their conditional probabilities.
